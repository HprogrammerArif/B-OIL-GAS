The prototype must look like an authentic, premium in-house product of **Bashundhara Group**, embodying prestige, industrial authority, and modern digital elegance.

```

```

### Typography & Iconography
- **Headings & Executive Numbers:** *Outfit* or *Inter* (Semi-Bold / Bold), clean tracking.
- **Body & Data Grids:** *Inter* for dense data legibility.
- **Bengali Typography:** *Hind Siliguri* or *Noto Sans Bengali* for clean, modern Bengali script display.
- **Iconography:** Lucide Icons / Heroicons (stroke width 1.75px) in Slate and Brand Crimson.

## 3. Step-by-Step Live Demo Presentation Script

### Act 1: The Chairman Executive Command Center (00:00 - 05:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** The Chairman Command Center opens with the iconic Bashundhara logo, a greeting banner, and high-level health cards.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Point out the **Live Enterprise Indicators**: Total Headcount Present (e.g., 94.2% across HQ & Keraniganj Plant), 3 Pending Urgent Approvals, and 1 License Requiring Board Action.
  2. Scroll to the **"Inner Circle" Executive Roster**:
     * Show live statuses: *Engr. M. Rahman (Plant GM - Present at Keraniganj)*, *K. Ahmed (Head of Legal - In Court Hearing)*, *S. Haque (CFO - In Meeting)*.
  3. Click **"Publish Chairman Broadcast"**:
     * Show modal to compose an Executive Directive in English/Bengali with Gold seal styling.
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"Chairman Sir, this view is your digital cockpit. In 5 seconds, before taking your morning tea, you know the pulse of the refinery, the location of your senior leadership, and any regulatory or safety issues requiring your intervention."*

### Act 2: Digital Note-Sheet & E-Signature Studio (05:00 - 10:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** A procurement file: *"Requisition for High-Pressure Safety Valves - Keraniganj Plant (Value: 4,850,000 BDT)"*.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Show the attached PDF blueprint.
  2. Open the **"Digital Note-Sheet"** tab:
     * Point out Paragraph 1 from Plant Engineer, Paragraph 2 from DGM Procurement, Paragraph 3 from Head of Accounts.
  3. Type a brief comment in the review box: *"Approved within FY26 maintenance budget."*
  4. Click **"Affix E-Signature"**:
     * Draw signature or select stored official seal.
     * Click **"Generate Tamper-Proof Certificate"**:
     * System displays the verifiable SHA-256 hash, IP address, and QR verification stamp.
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"Instead of physical file folders sitting on desks for 10 days while people travel between Bashundhara HQ and the refinery, files move instantaneously with complete legal note-sheet remarks, OTP security, and cryptographic audit certificates."*

### Act 3: Regulatory Compliance & License Pipeline (10:00 - 15:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** The 5-stage visual Kanban pipeline for statutory compliance.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Highlight the card: *"BSTI Quality Certification - Gas Cylinder Testing Unit"* (Flagged Yellow: Expiry in 38 days).
  2. Click the card to open the **5-Stage Inspection Progress**:
     * Step 1: Fund Approval (Completed - 120,000 BDT).
     * Step 2: Application Submitted (Completed - Challan #BSTI-9921 attached).
     * Step 3: Authority Site Inspection (Scheduled for October 24, 2026).
  3. Drag the card forward or click *"Log Audit Inspection Findings"*.
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"In the oil and gas industry, a single lapsed license from the Department of Explosives or BSTI can halt operations. Our due-date engine begins a 90-day countdown automatically, ensuring budget, paperwork, and inspection readiness with zero human forgetfulness."*

### Act 4: Chain-of-Custody Parcel Tracker (15:00 - 18:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** Parcel #BOGCL-TRK-8821: *"Confidential Lab Analysis Samples - Keraniganj Refinery to Central Lab"*.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Show the 5-point milestone progress bar: `Dispatched -> Gatekeeper Scanned -> In Transit with Driver -> Arrived at Destination -> Recipient Confirmed`.
  2. Click **"Simulate QR Handoff Scan"**:
     * Live animation changes custody from Driver to Receiving Officer with timestamp and GPS coordinates.
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"No more phone calls asking 'did the driver deliver the parcel?'. Every critical sample, contract, and spare part has a verifiable chain of custody."*

### Act 5: Fleet Dispatch & Smart Passenger Pooling (18:00 - 22:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** Vehicle Requisition Dashboard.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Click **"New Requisition"**: Select *HQ to Keraniganj Plant at 11:00 AM*.
  2. System shows an intelligent prompt: *"Notice: Officer Tariqul Islam is also traveling to Keraniganj Plant at 11:15 AM in Vehicle DHA-METRO-CHA-11-2045. Pool Ride to save fuel?"*
  3. Show the Transport Admin dispatch calendar with assigned drivers and vehicle fitness status.
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"Smart pooling alone reduces fuel consumption and vehicle wear by up to 20%, while ensuring that plant travel is strictly verified by odometer readings."*

### Act 6: Commuter Bus Seat Reservation (FIFO Waitlist) (22:00 - 25:00)
* **Visual on Screen:** Route #3: *Uttara to Keraniganj Plant (20/20 Seats Occupied)*.
* **Demonstrator Actions:**
  1. Show the visual bus seating layout (Seats 1 to 20 all green/assigned).
  2. Show the **FIFO Waitlist**:
     * Rank #1: Tanvir Ahmed (Applied 12 Sep 2026).
     * Rank #2: Mustafizur Rahman (Applied 28 Sep 2026).
  3. Click **"Simulate Seat Vacation"** (Employee transfers or resigns):
     * Seat #14 turns amber (`OFFERED_TO_WAITLIST`).
     * A simulated push notification pops up for Tanvir Ahmed: *"Seat #14 is available for Route #3. You have 48 hours to claim."*
* **Presenter Script:**
  > *"This transparent, algorithmic waitlist ends employee favoritism and disputes. Everyone knows their queue position, and seats are automatically re-allocated the second someone leaves."*
