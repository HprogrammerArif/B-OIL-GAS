## 3. Departmental Discovery Questionnaires

Use these structured interview questions during the discovery sessions with each department head:

### 3.1 Chairman & Managing Director’s Office
1. **Reporting Metrics:** What are the top 5 operational metrics the Chairman expects to review every morning? (e.g., Daily refinery throughput, plant attendance percentage, critical safety incidents, high-value procurement requests).
2. **Inner Circle Roster:** Which key directors, advisors, and general managers must be tracked on the executive presence board?
3. **Emergency Broadcasts:** What is the protocol for emergency factory alerts versus ceremonial holiday greetings? Should the Chairman's video addresses be delivered with mandatory push notifications?
4. **Delegation of Authority:** What financial ceiling can department heads approve before a file automatically routes to the Chairman's desk?

### 3.2 Human Resources & Administration
1. **Attendance Policies:** What are the current shift rosters (Day, Night, General) at Keraniganj Plant versus Head Office?
2. **Geofence Tolerances:** What is the allowed grace period for late arrivals? How do you currently handle field sales officers or depot workers traveling outside HQ?
3. **Bus Seat Waitlist:** How many commuter bus routes currently operate? How frequently do employees surrender their seats, and how are disputes handled today?
4. **Existing Systems:** Is there an existing biometric fingerprint machine or payroll software (e.g., customized ERP or Excel) that BOGCL OneOps must synchronize with?

### 3.3 Legal, Compliance & Regulatory Affairs
1. **Statutory Register:** Can we obtain the master inventory of all current operating licenses? (Department of Explosives, BSTI, Fire Service, Department of Environment, Trade Licenses, Boiler Inspectorate).
2. **Renewal Lead Times:** Which license requires the longest government inspection lead time? (Usually Explosives or BSTI, requiring 60-90 days).
3. **Fee Approvals:** What is the treasury chalan payment workflow for regulatory renewal fees?
4. **Audit History:** What documentation do government inspectors request during unexpected site audits?

### 3.4 HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) & Plant Operations
1. **Incident Severity Standards:** Do you follow OSHA guidelines or an internal Bashundhara Safety Manual for classifying Near Misses vs Lost Time Injuries (LTI)?
2. **Emergency Alarms:** When a hazardous gas leak or fire occurs at the refinery or depot, who must receive immediate automated phone/SMS alerts?
3. **Equipment Calibration:** Which critical refinery assets (pressure relief valves, gas chromatographs, storage tank level sensors) require mandatory periodic calibration?

### 3.5 Transport, Fleet & Logistics
1. **Fleet Composition:** How many pool vehicles, microbuses, and fuel tankers does BOGCL operate?
2. **Requisition Priority:** When multiple executives request vehicles simultaneously, what is the priority order?
3. **Fuel Audit:** How is fuel reconciled today? (Fuel cards, physical chalan from designated pumps, or cash receipts?).

### 3.6 Finance, Accounts & Internal Audit
1. **TA/DA Grade Entitlements:** What are the exact daily allowance ceilings across employee grades (Officer, Assistant Manager, DGM, GM, Director)?
2. **Advance Adjustments:** What is the maximum duration permitted for an employee to settle a tour advance after returning to office?
3. **Payment Disbursement:** Will approved conveyance claims be disbursed via bank transfer advice, petty cash, or credited directly to monthly payroll?

## 4. Tough Objections & Proven Winning Responses

### Objection 1: *"Why shouldn't we just purchase SAP, Oracle, or an off-the-shelf system like Odoo?"*
* **Winning Answer:**  
  > *"Enterprise ERPs like SAP and Oracle are world-class for financial accounting and general ledger, but they are notoriously rigid, slow to modify, and cost millions in annual per-seat licensing fees. Furthermore, standard SAP cannot handle Bangladesh-specific realities—such as the handwritten note-sheet approval culture, local BSTI/Explosives regulatory workflows, rickshaw/CNG conveyance receipts, and our specific commuter bus waitlist rules.  
  > BOGCL OneOps is designed to sit comfortably on top of your existing systems—acting as the modern, agile operational front door that your employees will actually love using, while keeping your data 100% owned by Bashundhara Group."*

### Objection 2: *"Is a digital e-signature legally valid in Bangladesh for our official documents?"*
* **Winning Answer:**  
  > *"Yes. Under the Bangladesh Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Act of 2006 (amended 2013), electronic signatures are recognized for internal corporate governance, memos, and operational contracts.  
  > To ensure 100% legal enforceability and internal audit compliance, BOGCL OneOps does not merely stamp an image of a signature. Every signed document generates an immutable Tamper-Proof Audit Certificate embedding the signer’s unique Employee ID, cryptographic SHA-256 hash, IP address, and timestamp. If even a single punctuation mark is changed in the PDF, the certificate invalidates immediately."*

### Objection 3: *"What about our gatekeepers, plant security, and drivers who are not comfortable with English?"*
* **Winning Answer:**  
  > *"We engineered BOGCL OneOps with a dual-engine architecture: English for corporate management and 100% native Bengali (বাংলা) with large, visual touch targets for plant personnel. A gatekeeper or driver simply taps large, unmistakable buttons with clear icons—such as 'পার্সেল গ্রহণ' (Receive Parcel) or 'ট্রিপ শুরু' (Start Trip)—with zero training required."*

### Objection 4: *"Can employees fake their attendance using Mock GPS or VPN location apps?"*
* **Winning Answer:**  
  > *"We implemented strict anti-spoofing protocols. The mobile app detects operating system mock-location flags, developer debugging modes, and jailbroken/rooted devices. Furthermore, the coordinates are cross-verified server-side using the Haversine mathematical algorithm against the exact geo-perimeter of Keraniganj Plant and Bashundhara HQ."*
