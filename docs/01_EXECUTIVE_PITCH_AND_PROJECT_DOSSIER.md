## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Intent

Bashundhara Oil and Gas Company Limited (BOGCL), an elite enterprise of Bashundhara Group, represents one of Bangladesh’s largest investments in the energy and downstream petroleum sector. Managing massive refining capacity, depots across the nation, high-value industrial assets, cross-district logistics, hundreds of compliance obligations, and a massive workforce requires an uncompromising level of operational discipline, governance, and transparency.

Currently, key enterprise workflows—ranging from internal note-sheets and parcel movements to vehicle dispatches, compliance renewals (BSTI, Department of Explosives), and travel expense settlements—rely on disparate paper trails, spreadsheets, manual phone follow-ups, and fragmented communication.

### 2.2 Cultural & Operational Realities of Downstream Oil & Gas in Bangladesh
Operating in Bangladesh's petroleum sector involves unique friction points that generic western software (like basic Slack, Trello, or off-the-shelf SaaS) cannot handle:
1. **The "Note-Sheet" Heritage:** In large Bangladeshi conglomerates and government liaison work, decisions follow the traditional note-sheet method (file notes moving up the hierarchy with initials, marginal comments, and official seals). BOGCL OneOps introduces the **Digital Note-Sheet** alongside visual document approval.
2. **High Regulatory Stakes:** Oil & Gas facilities require constant renewals with the **Department of Explosives (DoE)**, **BSTI**, **Fire Service & Civil Defence**, **Department of Environment (DoE)**, and **BIWTA**. A missed deadline means plant shutdown, heavy penalties, or regulatory scrutiny. BOGCL OneOps transforms compliance from reactive firefighting into an automated 90-day forward countdown pipeline.
3. **Safety & Zero-Tolerance HSE:** Petrochemical refining and LPG handling are inherently hazardous. HSE incident tracking with root-cause analysis (CAPA) protects lives, plant infrastructure, and corporate reputation.
4. **Bilingual Workforce:** While executives work in English, gatekeepers, security personnel, depot supervisors, and drivers require native **Bangla (বাংলা)** language interfaces with intuitive icons and large touch targets.

## 6. Hosting, Security & Enterprise Data Sovereignty

As an oil and gas producer of critical national importance in Bangladesh:
- **Hosting Flexibility:** Dockerized container architecture capable of running **On-Premise** (at Bashundhara Corporate Data Center in Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka) or on a **Sovereign Tier-III Local Cloud** (e.g., Summit Communications, Felicity, or BDCOM).
- **Data Sovereignty:** All employee personal records, facial biometrics, HSE incident videos, and Chairman communications remain strictly within the geographical boundaries of Bangladesh.
- **Enterprise Security:**
  - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with strict organizational and departmental data isolation.
  - End-to-End TLS 1.3 encryption in transit; AES-256 encryption at rest for documents and database dumps.
  - SHA-256 cryptographic hashes embedded into every signed document certificate, guaranteeing anti-tamper validity.
  - Immutable Append-Only Audit Logging for all financial, compliance, and executive actions.
